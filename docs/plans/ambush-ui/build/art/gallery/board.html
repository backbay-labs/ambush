Perch / gallery

Drawn from the live control room. That world solved Perch’s hardest problem a century ago: many simultaneous states, one operator, a hard hierarchy, and an unambiguous signal for what is happening right now. It also owns the exact distinction the two-stroke grant turns on. A source on preview is cued and waiting for the take. A source on program is going out to everyone.

The law of this direction There is exactly one colour in Perch, and it is a lens over a lamp. If you can see any colour on the screen, an irreversible action is running in production right now. Everything else — every panel, every rule, every severity, every meter — is metal, black glass and light.

Every bay names its own source in the right cell of its under-monitor display. A string printed inside a slot that cannot name a fixture path does not belong on the screen — which is how the prototype’s network_connectivity and remote_management were caught: they appear nowhere in the fixture. The fixture says network.egress and network.ingress, and that is what slot 02 prints.

What the take bar does, kept out of the take bar. Perch does not authorize. Recording publishes a human intent record to the relay under your own key, carrying your Ed25519 signature over {decided_at_ms, decision, hold_id}, and separately posts your decision to the daemon, which re-derives authority from scratch — partition, governance, policy, rate limit — and mints the capability lease. The two legs cross a process boundary, which is what makes this guaranteed rather than conventional. That paragraph is a specification, so it lives out here in the margin: on a 3am bench every sentence between the operator and the blast radius is a cost.

Measured, live, from the tokens

Every figure is recomputed from the live computed styles each time the theme changes, so the table cannot drift from the tokens it describes. dE2000 is against all 36 banned values; contrast is WCAG 2.x on the real surface each ink actually sits on.

Notes

The floor, and where it stops. The 10 dE2000 floor is applied to chromatic tokens. There is one, and it clears by 12.92. It is not applied to low-chroma neutrals, because at L* 84–92 every near-white in existence is within 10 dE2000 of #e2e8f0 — that value is itself a near-white, and the only way to “clear” it is to add chroma the room does not have. Choosing a hue by subtraction from a banned list is the failure this whole exercise is about. The evidence that the warm hue was not aimed at anything: the dark ramp, where there is room to stand, clears 14.5–15.9 without anyone trying.

The law, audited.

Encoded without colour. Severity is a word, a count and four segments. Confidence is a two-decimal numeral and five segments. The verification tier is its number, the word UNATTESTED and a hatched NO SIGNAL plate. Hatching means one thing everywhere on this board: absent, or forbidden.

A control never carries a lamp. In a gallery the tally lamps are on the monitors and the take bar is a bare machined lever on the desk. That rule — taken from the world, not from a critique — is why the destructive control has no chromatic fill anywhere in it, and why its state is a position.

Type is rem-derived from a single --rem; there is no px or arbitrary-rem size in this file. Archivo, Archivo Narrow and IBM Plex Mono are all on Google Fonts and Fontsource and are self-hostable; this board fetches nothing and is designed so the fallback rendering is the one that must look right.
