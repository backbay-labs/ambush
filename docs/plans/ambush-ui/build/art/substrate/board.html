Perch — Substrate

drawn from the spot plate: a reagent read by eye, and what its depth means

Ambush detects by accumulation. Signed observations decay on a 3 600-second half-life; concentration is the sum of what is still live; posture changes when that sum crosses a threshold with enough distinct sources. That is a titration, and the instrument for a titration is a white tile you read the colour depth on. So in Perch, colour is a measurement — one permanganate ladder, meaning position on the decay axis, drawn dark-on-light on a bone plate. Severity, urgency, danger, verification and undo are carried achromatically, because not one of them is something the substrate measured.

The ladder · five values, one meaning

Dilute permanganate is a faint rose; concentrated it is a near-black violet. That progression is the one this product needs, it is nameable material rather than a hex stepped away from a framework, and it is the reason the darkest, gravest value in the system is not a red. Every step clears ΔE2000 15 from the nearest banned value; the closest is 15.29.

Where colour is allowed to exist

The ladder never touches the bench. It appears only on the plate — glazed porcelain, #E8E4D0, a warm bone tile inset into a cold-free, faintly warm matte black. A stain gets darker as it concentrates, so on the plate the ladder runs dark-on-light, which is what accumulation actually looks like.

Because the plate is the same bone in light mode as in dark, all five chromatic values are theme-invariant. Not one colour is declared inside a media query; only the nine bench neutrals move.

A single hue family varying evenly in lightness — L* 54 down to 24, seven and a half steps apart — survives every form of colour-blindness and a greyscale monitor, and it is redundant anyway: every ladder prints its figure, rules its thresholds, and outlines every cell so a faint lit cell can never read as an unlit one.

Type

Source Serif 4 for every sentence a human reads, with Iowan Old Style / Charter behind it. IBM Plex Mono for every identifier, figure, label and control, with SF Mono / Menlo behind it. Both self-host from Fontsource; neither is fetched, and this board renders entirely on the fallbacks. No sans-serif appears anywhere in Perch: a record is set in a text face, a measurement is set in a machine face, and the product contains nothing else. Six type steps, all rem, floored at 0.6875rem — including every label inside the SVG figures.

The signature · the two plates

One instrument, at two sizes and nowhere else: 66 px as a six-cell chip on a queue row and a lane, 812 units across the verdict pane. Base 2 from the evaporation floor to 8, so one octave is one half-life and distance along the axis is elapsed time.

At full size it states a fact no other instrument on the screen can reach. Perch's hold TTL is 3 600 s. The substrate's half-life is 3 600 s. They are the same number. A hold left undecided expires at the exact moment the concentration that opened it has halved — and it will have stood beneath its own alert threshold for the final 34 minutes of that hour.

And there is a second plate. The control that isolates a production host is a plate bed: an empty marked recess until you arm it, bone once the plate seats, written on once you record. Two objects in this product may be lit — the measurement, and the irreversible act — and there is never a third.

The order never varies

Action, blast radius, if you undo, why we are asking, what granting opens — in that order for every action type, so the position of a fact is itself a fact. A slot that cannot be filled prints ABSENT and its reason inside a dashed frame; it never collapses, because a missing row and an absent row look identical once the row is gone. That is why the pane is drawn here in one piece rather than cut across a page: the fixed order is the mechanism, and a mechanism you have to turn a page to verify is not one.

Why the queue is not on this screen

Adjudication takes the whole working surface. The queue is the thing that makes you hurry, and hurry is the failure mode this pane exists to prevent — so opening a hold puts it away, and Esc brings it back. The consequence for this drawing is that the pane is one column at its real length, with no second column standing half empty beside it.

Severity, at two scales, in no colour

The rail down the left edge of the card is four segments filled from the top — the same four cells as the chip in the header, at the scale of the whole pane. It is the only full-height object on the screen, it is --ink on --void at 17.9:1, and it is countable. CRITICAL is four.

And it is grey on purpose. Slot 04 says why: severity and threat_class are read out of the requesting agent's own request. A claim and a measurement cannot share an encoding — so the claim gets cells and a word, and the measurement gets the ladder.

Two kinds of untrustworthy string

Adversary-written values carry the hatch. Agent-written values — severity, threat_class — carry the word request-carried instead: not attacker text, but not the daemon's either. Two different problems, two different marks, and neither of them is a colour.

The blast radius comes before the measurement

An earlier draft put the plate directly under the header, because it is the most beautiful object on the page and that is where a designer puts one. It inverted the safety hierarchy: the cost of the action has to be read before its justification. The plate now sits inside slot 04, the slot it is evidence for, and the header carries the reading at chip scale instead.

The clock is a subtraction

One agent, two detectors

One octave is one hour

The axis is base 2 from the evaporation floor 0.01 to 8, so equal width is equal time and the ramp bands D1–D4 are exactly one hour wide each. ALERT 2.0 is a cell boundary because the threshold is a power of two. INCIDENT 5.0 is not, so it is ruled explicitly rather than faked into the ramp.

Below the first band the substrate is still live but not yet worth a step, so that region is hatched, not coloured. Beneath the evaporation floor the plate is simply blank — the one place in this system where absence of mark means absence of fact.

What the ladder cannot say

Concentration is a measurement, and it is the only one on the screen. It does not know whether this host matters, whether it is 3am, or whether the last four holds were false. The ladder is deliberately not a priority score: it reports the one number that was reduced from signed, decaying, multi-source evidence, and leaves the judgment where it belongs. That is the risk this direction takes — the loudest object on the pane is a fact rather than an opinion.

Absence renders

Two absences here, both drawn rather than dropped: a governance receipt that is not carried on this path, and — on the Watch — a Named you queue that does not exist because no operator directory exists in either tree. Neither collapses, and neither is reported as a zero.

Two leases, two clocks

A 60 s capability lease minted at your decision, and a 900 s containment lease opened on dispatch. Unrelated objects with unrelated clocks, so slot 05 prints them as two facts and never as one progress bar.

The tier is honest about nothing

No shield, no lock, no tick, and no colour. Three named checks, the count 0 of 3, the number tier 0, and the sentence “unattested — no ed25519 signature of its own”, hatched the way adversary strings are. The one affordance offered is the only real one: re-read the record and diff the bytes.

The same five hexes

Density means distance from the plate, and the plate is the same bone in either theme. So the ladder is byte-identical here and on Plate I; nothing about the ramp is defined inside a media query, and there is no light-mode ramp to keep in sync — which also means there is no second danger hex to get wrong at 3am.

What moved, and why it moved this far

VOID → #C1BEBA, SURFACE → #CBC8C4, CARD → #D5D2CE, INSET → #CFCCC8, LINE → #ABA8A3, LINE-2 → #8E8B87, INK → #1F1D1B, INK-2 → #3B3835, INK-3 → #4E4B47. Nine values.

The light bench is grounded at L* 77–85, not 95–99. A theme that goes to paper white puts the plate at the same value as the page and the whole picture inverts out of existence. At L* 77–85 the tile is still the lightest and warmest thing on the bench, and it still has an edge and a highlight, so it still reads as an object lying on a surface rather than a grey rectangle printed on one.

The bed inverts with the room

Because the plate bed is drawn in --void and --plate rather than in a hue, the empty state stays the darkest thing on the screen in either theme and the seated state stays bone. A colour-coded danger button would have needed a second hex here.

Honest about one thing

In the dark, the plate is the one lit object in an unlit room, and that is the whole image. Under work light nothing can be the one lit object. So in this theme the plate stops being identified by luminance and is identified by temperature and edge — it is the warm glazed thing on a cold matte bench. That is a translation, not the same picture, and it is worth saying out loud rather than pretending otherwise.

The discipline, stated once

Five chromatic values. One ladder. One meaning. D1 through D5 are one octave apart on a base-2 scale of live strength, and the fifth begins at the alert threshold. Nothing else in the product is coloured — not severity, not urgency, not the destructive control, not an error, not a success. Two further ramps exist and neither is chromatic: the bench (nine neutrals, all C* < 2.8) and the plate (four tinted neutrals of one hue, C* 3–10). Six values by the strictest count, of a budget of eight.

Severity has no colour on purpose. severity and threat_class are read out of the requesting agent's own request; concentration is the substrate's own reduction over signed, decaying deposits from distinct sources. One is a claim and one is a measurement, and only measurements are coloured. Severity gets four cells in the header, four segments down the full height of the card, the word, and the note that somebody claimed it.

Everything survives without colour. Severity: cells, rail, word. Confidence: dots plus figure. Verification tier: three named checks, a count, a number and a sentence. Concentration: a printed figure, ruled thresholds, and outlined cells — and because the ladder is evenly spaced in L*, it survives greyscale as a ramp too. Adversary-controlled text: a hatch, not a hue. Absence: a dashed frame and the word, never a collapse.

Shading is not colour. The only non-token fills anywhere in this document are black and white at low alpha — an inset shadow in the bed, a specular highlight along the top of every plate. No gradient is used as an accent.
