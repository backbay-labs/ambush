# Milestone v1.36 Audit

## Summary

`v1.36 SIEM/SOAR Forward And Alert Routing` achieved its definition of done. The runtime now forwards canonical `swarm_finding` payloads to Splunk, ELK, or Chronicle through resilient execution, enriches every finding before persistence and delivery, routes alerts to named notification channels through repo-owned rules, and lets operators replay suppressed notifications from authenticated dead-letter endpoints.
