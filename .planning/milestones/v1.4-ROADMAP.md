## Milestone Summary

### Key Decisions

- Keep adversarial suites and candidate detectors manifest-driven and repo-owned.
- Reuse the replay harness for suite execution and experiment evaluation instead of building a parallel bench runtime.
- Treat adversarial scenario misses as the first known-bad coverage signal and benign controls as the false-positive signal.

### Issues Encountered

- The first detector-profile refactor accidentally derived `Eq` over floating-point thresholds; removing that derive fixed the build.
- Parallel verification commands briefly serialized on Cargo’s shared cache lock but completed cleanly.

### Tech Debt

- Experiment gates are still heuristic and replay-driven; formal proof or promotion workflows remain future work.
- Known-bad coverage is defined by tracked adversarial scenarios, not a dedicated proof corpus yet.
