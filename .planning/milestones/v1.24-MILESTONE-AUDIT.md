# Milestone v1.24 Audit

## Summary

`v1.24 Approval Ledger And Quorum Readiness` achieved its definition of done. The repo now supports local approval sets, signed approval ledgers, deterministic approval verdicts, portable signed receipt packs, and human-gated production promotions for critical severity without introducing distributed consensus.

## Requirements Coverage

| Requirement | Phase | Verification | Final Status | Evidence |
|-------------|-------|--------------|--------------|----------|
| GOV-03 | 75 | passed | satisfied | Operators can now create approval sets with eligible voters, threshold rules, and promotion evidence refs through the runtime harness and `swarmctl`. |
| GOV-04 | 75 | passed | satisfied | Approval ledgers now persist signed vote lineage, explicit quorum state, and approval-set linkage. |
| GOV-05 | 76 | passed | satisfied | Deterministic approval verdicts now evaluate approval ledgers against configured threshold rules. |
| GOV-06 | 76 | passed | satisfied | Signed portable receipt packs now preserve approval set, ledger, verdict, audit refs, content hash, and detached signature material. |
| GOV-07 | 77 | passed | satisfied | Critical-severity promotions now persist explicit human-approval-pending review packets and fail closed until approval. |
| GOV-01 | 77 | passed | satisfied | Promotion approval now includes a structural quorum gate ready to activate without changing the promotion model. |
| GOV-02 | 77 | passed | satisfied | Promotion records now preserve signed vote references and optional durable consensus receipts. |

- Existing promotion read surfaces were sufficient for pending-review inspection, so no second promotion-specific HTTP service was introduced.
