## Cross-Phase Integration

The milestone contract is coherent end to end:

- Phase 172 turned evasion coverage floors and solver proof outcomes into one repo-owned assurance policy with explicit blocked decisions.
- Phase 173 converted blocked assurance outcomes into durable replay-ready assurance cases and fed that lineage back into mutation ranking and review summaries.
- Phase 174 made queue review, handoff creation, canary admission, and promotion start fail closed on unsatisfied assurance lineage instead of treating robustness artifacts as advisory only.
- Phase 175 added signed bounded waivers and surfaced the resulting lineage through the same proposal, handoff, canary, promotion, and runtime-status artifacts instead of inventing a parallel override channel.

## E2E Flows Verified

1. Coverage floors and solver proof outcomes now generate one explicit assurance decision that can block rollout before queue acceptance or handoff launch.
2. Coverage misses and solver counterexamples now regenerate replayable assurance cases with durable lineage back to the triggering candidate and proof artifacts.
3. Queue, handoff, canary, and promotion all fail closed on unsatisfied assurance lineage unless a valid active bounded waiver is attached directly to that lineage.
4. Shared review and status surfaces now expose assurance state, waived gaps, waiver identity, expiry, and reason without bypassing the normal shipped rollout contract.
