# Milestone v1.21: Cross-Lane Promotion Review

**Status:** COMPLETE
**Date:** 2026-04-04
**Milestone Goal:** Unify governance-prep, canary, and production evidence into one advisory cross-lane review flow without widening into quorum control or direct rollout authority.

## Overview

`v1.20` added durable evidence workbench sessions, export snapshots, and bounded review-driven maintenance handoffs above the authenticated operator API. That work closed the single-session comparison gap, but promotion review was still fragmented across separate governance-prep, canary, and production evidence lanes.

`v1.21` closed that lane-fragmentation gap. It added lane-aware review sessions that span governance-prep, canary, and production evidence, durable cross-lane comparison snapshots over the existing signed evidence stores, and one advisory promotion-readiness artifact that stays above the existing rollout and maintenance gates. Quorum approvals, signed vote exchange across independent nodes, and multi-user control remain deferred.

## Phase Plan

**Status:** COMPLETE

**Requirements:** OPS-23
