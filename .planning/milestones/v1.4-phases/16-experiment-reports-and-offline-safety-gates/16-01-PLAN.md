# Phase 16 Plan 01: Experiment Reports And Offline Safety Gates

<objective>
Turn offline detector experiments into a practical safety gate with persisted lineage, threshold verdicts, and operator-facing regression attribution.
</objective>

<read_first>
- .planning/phases/16-experiment-reports-and-offline-safety-gates/16-CONTEXT.md

- crates/swarm-runtime/src/replay.rs
- docs/CONFIGURATION.md
- experiments/
</read_first>

<tasks>
<task number="1" type="auto">
<description>Formalize the offline experiment gate verdicts and persist the report shape.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Reports include lineage, corpus version, and summary metrics.
- Gate verdicts cover known-bad coverage, false-positive delta, and latency delta.
- Reports persist under a dedicated experiment store.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task number="2" type="auto">
<description>Surface attributed regressions by scenario and technique group.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Failing benign controls and missed adversarial detections are called out separately.
- Technique-group regressions are derived from suite metadata.
- Rendered output is legible without opening raw JSON.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task number="3" type="auto">
<description>Document the suite and experiment workflow end to end and verify both pass and fail behavior.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Docs explain the named suite path, experiment manifests, result persistence, and nonzero failure semantics.
- One control experiment passes.
- One broadened candidate fails the false-positive gate as documented.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>
</tasks>
