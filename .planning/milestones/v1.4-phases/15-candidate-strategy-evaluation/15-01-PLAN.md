Add a repo-owned baseline-vs-candidate detector comparison path that stays fully offline and reuses the same replay suite for both sides.
</objective>

<tasks>
<task number="1" type="auto">
<description>Add a serializable detector profile and repo-owned experiment manifest format.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Candidate detector profiles can be declared in YAML.
- The production detector remains the baseline source of truth.
- Lineage metadata is preserved with every experiment manifest.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task number="2" type="auto">
<description>Execute baseline and candidate detectors against the same suite and persist the comparison report.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- One command evaluates baseline and candidate side by side.
- Reports capture detection rate, false positive rate, and max detect latency.
- Reports can be reloaded later by stable experiment ID.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task number="3" type="auto">
<description>Add tests and tracked manifests for a passing control candidate and a failing broadened candidate.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Focused tests cover persistence and at least one failing experiment gate.
- The control experiment passes end to end.
- The broadened candidate produces an attributed regression on the benign Python control.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>
</tasks>

</verification>

- Candidate detector manifests are repo-owned and offline-only.
- Baseline-vs-candidate comparison reports are durable and attributable.
