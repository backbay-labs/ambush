# Phase 9 Plan 01: Correlation And Incident Assembly

<objective>
Add deterministic incident assembly on top of persisted investigation bundles, with durable incident artifacts and explicit inclusion or rejection reasoning.
</objective>

<tasks>
<task number="1" type="auto">
<description>Add correlation configuration and durable incident artifact storage.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Correlation time-window and persistence settings are repository-owned.
- Incident records persist with hunt, investigation, receipt, and shared-key metadata.
- Memory and local-files incident stores mirror the established durability pattern.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task number="2" type="auto">
<description>Build a deterministic correlation engine that records inclusion and rejection reasons.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Incident assembly uses shared correlation keys and bounded time windows.
- Each candidate investigation is recorded as included or rejected with a stable reason.
- Incident summaries and metadata are reproducible from persisted inputs.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task number="3" type="auto">
<description>Add runtime helpers and tests for persisted incident assembly.</description>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Runtime helpers can assemble and persist an incident from stored investigation bundles.
- Operators can load incidents by incident ID or included hunt ID.
- Tests cover both successful grouping and rejected candidates.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>
</tasks>
