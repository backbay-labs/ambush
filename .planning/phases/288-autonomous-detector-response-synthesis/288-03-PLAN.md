<interfaces>
Response synthesis consumes only the single Runtime-owned
`ArenaSynthesisInput` from `synthesis/arena_input.rs`, including its
`PairReportView`/`ArenaArtifactView`; it never defines another input DTO or
imports concrete Arena records. For actionable Blue results it retains the
typed view of the full `BlueOutcome` policy verdict, `DryRunTraversalReceipt`,
`ResponseRehearsalPreview`, rollback, blast-radius, `Phase286InvestigationCapture`
and `PairReport` digests; it does not use `RedOutcomeProjection` as a substitute.
The existing `RuntimeService::playbook_preview(ResponsePlaybookPreviewRequest,
prepared_at_ms)` is the only action-resolution seam and must be pure. The
six-argument Phase 286 stack and `RuntimeService::rehearse_selected_simulation`
belong to the Phase 287 evidence producer; Phase 288 reuses their evidence and
never constructs an `ActionRequest` or invokes a response adapter.
</interfaces>
