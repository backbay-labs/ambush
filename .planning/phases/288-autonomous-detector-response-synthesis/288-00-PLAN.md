owner of the pure `ArenaSynthesisInput`, `PairReportView`, and
`ArenaArtifactView` DTOs. `contracts.rs` owns only reusable source-role and
canonicalization primitives; it must not define a second input or concrete
Arena records. `swarm-arena` owns Blue runs, CAS/fencing, and the concrete

forbidden. The adapter must validate the full `SignedStateEnvelope` expectation
(kind, signer, stream, sequence, generation, predecessor digest, fencing token,
payload digest, signature, and schema) before constructing the DTO.
