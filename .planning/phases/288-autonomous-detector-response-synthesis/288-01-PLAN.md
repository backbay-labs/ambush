families: actual `SignedBlueLearnedState`/`BlueRuntimeAdapter` evaluation in

`DetectorStrategyFamily::{SuspiciousProcessTree, BehavioralAnomaly,
FilelessExecution, DnsExfiltration}` values. The six additional candidate
families are factory-only and round-trip-tested here; they are rejected as
learned-state inputs until a concrete upstream Arena enum/path is extended.
