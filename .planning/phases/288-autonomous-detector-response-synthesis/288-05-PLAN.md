references; they do not pretend that a Runtime `ArenaSynthesisInput` owns an
Arena adapter. Candidate/profile/event/verdict controls use the same concrete
`BlueRuntimeAdapter::run` bridge/evaluator as the clean candidate and
`SignedBlueLearnedState::empty_frozen()` control. The one source-adapter
mutation follows the existing Phase 287 direct process seam exactly:
`ArenaDetectionStrategyAdapter<D>::new(inner)` is passed to the exact async
`IngestState::process_bridge_event_at<D,P,E>(&self, TelemetryEvent, &dyn
GraphClock, &ArenaDetectionStrategyAdapter<D>,
&ConfiguredRuntimeStack<P,E,Phase286StrategyBridge>) ->
Result<ArenaIngestResult, String>` (with the Phase 287 `DetectionStrategy`,
`ApprovalGate`, and `ResponseExecutor` bounds), after the same
`FixtureTarget::normalize_event(&FixtureEvent, GraphLogicalTime)` and the same
six-argument stack composition. It clones the enclosing config, mutates only
its admitted typed `DetectionConfig`, constructs the concrete detector with
`swarm_ingest_runtime::control::build_composite_detector(&mutated_config.detection)`,
then wraps that exact `ArenaIngestResult` with
`BlueOutcome::from_ingest_result(ArenaIngestResult,
Option<DryRunTraversalReceipt>)` and sends the resulting concrete outcome
through Arena-owned CAS/evaluation to obtain a full `PairReport`. It must not
define a provider trait, inject a detector into `BlueRuntimeAdapter::run`, or
claim a fallback composition hook. Every concrete `ArenaIngestResult`,
`Phase286InvestigationCapture`, `BlueOutcome`, `ArenaArtifactStore` record, and
`PairReport` remains Arena-owned and is converted once to before/after Runtime
views/digests. No control may use a copied lane fixture, generic replay harness,
Red projection, or independent score path.
