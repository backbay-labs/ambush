# Queued Milestone: v1.51 Assurance-Gated Evolution And Counterexample Loop

**Status:** Queued (activates after v1.50 completes)
**Created:** 2026-04-10

## Goal

Turn `v1.48` artifacts into real gate inputs by feeding evasion coverage, solver proofs, and counterexamples into queue, canary, promotion, and mutation decisions.

## Requirements

- **ASSURE-01**: evasion coverage floors as explicit gate inputs
- **ASSURE-02**: solver proof outcomes, timeouts, and counterexample presence as explicit gate inputs
- **ASSURE-03**: fail-closed queue, canary, and promotion behavior when assurance policy is unsatisfied
- **COUNTER-01**: harvested replay/regression cases from solver counterexamples and evasion misses
- **COUNTER-02**: harvested cases feed mutation ranking and proposal fitness
- **ASSURE-04**: signed bounded operator waivers with rationale
- **ASSURE-05**: surfaced assurance lineage across status, proof, and review artifacts

## Proposed Phase Structure

Starting from phase 172 (v1.50 ends at 171).

### Phase 172: Assurance Policy And Gate Inputs
**Goal**: Define assurance policy from coverage floors, solver outcomes, and fail-closed thresholds.
**Requirements**: ASSURE-01, ASSURE-02
**Success Criteria**:
1. Assurance thresholds are repo-owned and operator-readable.
2. Coverage and solver outcomes feed one shared policy instead of separate ad hoc checks.
3. Policy can explain why a candidate is blocked.

### Phase 173: Counterexample Harvest And Replay Regeneration
**Goal**: Convert evasion misses and solver counterexamples into replayable regression inputs with lineage.
**Requirements**: COUNTER-01, COUNTER-02
**Success Criteria**:
1. Counterexamples become durable replay cases.
2. Harvested cases link back to the strategy and proof that created them.
3. Mutation ranking and review summaries consume the harvested evidence.

### Phase 174: Assurance-Gated Queue, Canary, And Promotion
**Goal**: Apply assurance policy to the queue, canary, and promotion state machines.
**Requirements**: ASSURE-03
**Success Criteria**:
1. Queue, canary, and promotion all fail closed on unsatisfied assurance policy.
2. The runtime records the exact gate that blocked the candidate.
3. Existing rollout artifacts preserve assurance lineage.

### Phase 175: Waivers, Review Surface, And Assurance Lineage
**Goal**: Add bounded operator overrides and expose assurance lineage through the normal proof and review surfaces.
**Requirements**: ASSURE-04, ASSURE-05
**Success Criteria**:
1. Waivers are signed, reasoned, and time-bounded.
2. Review surfaces show which gaps were waived and why.
3. Assurance lineage is exportable with proof artifacts.
