# Queued Milestone: v1.50 Async Enrichment And Correlation Depth

**Status:** Queued (activates after v1.49 completes)
**Created:** 2026-04-10

## Goal

Make the async lane a first-class product surface by adding multi-graph Weaver correlation, investigation scheduling and confidence voting, and deeper behavioral baselines across host, identity, and peer-group scope.

## Requirements

- **ASYNC-01**: multi-graph Weaver traversal across temporal, causal, entity, and semantic dimensions
- **ASYNC-02**: explainable cross-hunt evidence chains and confidence scores for correlated incidents
- **SCHED-01**: priority-based async investigation scheduling with bounded queue budgets
- **SCHED-02**: speculate-plus-vote confidence workflow for ambiguous investigations
- **BEHAV-01**: identity and peer-group behavioral baselines
- **BEHAV-02**: per-scope baseline persistence and decay
- **ASYNC-03**: operator-visible async queue, backlog, and confidence-vote surfaces
- **ASYNC-04**: end-to-end async-lane integration proof

## Proposed Phase Structure

Starting from phase 168 (v1.49 ends at 167).

### Phase 168: Weaver Multi-Graph Correlation Foundations
**Goal**: Extend the current graph-backed correlation lane toward temporal, causal, entity, and semantic traversal.
**Requirements**: ASYNC-01, ASYNC-02
**Success Criteria**:
1. Correlation logic can traverse the four graph dimensions with repo-owned scoring rules.
2. Incidents include explainable evidence chains instead of opaque correlation summaries.
3. Cross-hunt stitching works without widening the hot path.

### Phase 169: Investigation Scheduling And Confidence Voting
**Goal**: Prioritize async investigations by value and capture competing interpretations for ambiguous leads.
**Requirements**: SCHED-01, SCHED-02
**Success Criteria**:
1. Investigation priority accounts for criticality, freshness, and learned value.
2. Ambiguous hunts can produce multiple candidate interpretations before final confidence is chosen.
3. Queue budgets and starvation behavior are explicit and testable.

### Phase 170: Identity And Peer-Group Behavioral Baselines
**Goal**: Extend behavioral learning from per-host scope into identity and peer-group scope.
**Requirements**: BEHAV-01, BEHAV-02
**Success Criteria**:
1. Host, identity, and peer-group baselines are tracked independently.
2. Baselines persist and decay by scope with repo-owned thresholds.
3. Findings explain which scope triggered the anomaly.

### Phase 171: Async Enrichment Surface And Integration Proof
**Goal**: Make the async lane operator-visible and prove the bounded detect → investigate → correlate flow end to end.
**Requirements**: ASYNC-03, ASYNC-04
**Success Criteria**:
1. Status surfaces expose async backlog, pressure, and correlation outcomes.
2. Integration proof covers detection to operator-visible correlated incident.
3. Async-lane failure modes stay bounded and visible.
