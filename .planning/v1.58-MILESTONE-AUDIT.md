  predicate matching seam without widening the hot-path contract.
- Phase 201 turned that substrate into a real sequence detector backed by
  repo-owned YAML rules with ATT&CK chain metadata.
- Phase 202 added three chain-only replay scenarios and a named suite proving
  the new coverage is not just a rephrasing of existing deterministic
  single-event heuristics.
- Phase 203 integrated partial and full sequence findings into the existing
  signed pheromone, replay, investigation, and incident lanes for both live
  service execution and offline replay.

## E2E Flows Verified

1. Accepted telemetry is retained in a bounded shared temporal window with

2. ATT&CK chain rules loaded from repo-owned YAML can emit partial and full
   findings against that shared retained-event substrate.
3. The three shipped chain-only replay scenarios stay quiet under the
   deterministic single-event detector set and pass when the sequence detector
   is active.
4. Sequence findings now persist through the normal deposit, replay,
   investigation, and incident-correlation lanes with strategy-scoped agent
   attribution and lower-confidence intermediate partial deposits.
