## Cross-Phase Integration

The milestone wiring is complete end to end:

- Phase 137 established a runtime-owned `KittenAgent`, repo-owned evolution config, and a bounded drift-triggered mutation state machine.
- Phase 138 made the evolution lane durable by scoring candidates against replay and verification evidence, persisting survivors, and enforcing restart-safe proposal throttling.
- Phase 139 converted proposal-ready candidates into real formal-safety-gated canary admissions through the existing selection, bridge, handoff, and canary lane.
- Phase 140 made that lane inspectable through a durable status summary, a typed runtime SSE event, and a concise `swarmctl evolution status` operator surface.

## E2E Flows Verified

1. Kitten can detect drift, materialize and validate candidates, and persist a replay-scored proposal-ready population.
2. A proposal-ready candidate is formally verified against repo-owned invariants, recorded into durable selection state, and launched into canary when admitted.
3. Rejected and blocked proposals remain observable through persisted review state rather than disappearing after routing.
4. Operators can inspect evolution status through both the existing runtime status surface and the dedicated `swarmctl evolution status` command.
