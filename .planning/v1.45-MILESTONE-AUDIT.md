## Cross-Phase Integration

The milestone wiring is complete end to end:

- Phase 149 established the shared Providence webhook contract, bearer auth, and HMAC signing surface.
- Phase 150 moved Providence delivery onto a dedicated lifecycle adapter with durable external references, retries, dead-lettering, and runtime health reporting.
- Phase 151 added signed inbound analyst feedback, substrate and investigation side effects, durable audit persistence, and negative supervision flow into Kitten or pending storage.
- Phase 152 packaged live scoped runtime visibility for Providence through the widget surface and short-lived read-only context tokens tied back to the same operator-owned trust boundary.

## E2E Flows Verified

1. Swarm now signs and delivers Providence incident lifecycle requests through one dedicated adapter and reports Providence health on `/healthz` and `/readyz`.
2. Providence analysts can send signed confirm, dismiss, and investigate actions back into Swarm, and those actions persist durable incident-linked audit evidence.
3. False-positive dismissals now influence the evolution lane through Kitten penalties or durable pending storage rather than stopping at the transport layer.
4. Providence can embed a live scoped widget and open read-only findings or incidents drilldowns through short-lived signed context tokens without holding full operator credentials.
