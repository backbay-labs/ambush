## Cross-Phase Integration

The milestone contract is coherent end to end:

- Phase 196 established bounded autonomous mutation generation from durable
  winners with replayable parent lineage instead of operator-authored specs.
- Phase 197 attached corpus-measured catch-rate, false-positive, and latency
  fitness to the resulting candidates and adversarial episodes.
- Phase 198 turned that measured lane into a reproducible N-generation
  benchmark with durable generation reports and a checked-in no-gain baseline.
- Phase 199 closed the loop by making the benchmark baseline-aware, adding a
  bounded gap-expansion recipe, and publishing one exact measured improvement
  claim against the tracked evasion corpus.

## E2E Flows Verified

1. Durable population winners can seed autonomous bounded mutation without
   widening the existing materialize, validate, rank, and refresh seams.
2. The bounded benchmark can persist both staged-baseline metrics and
   generation-over-generation results for later inspection.
3. A conservative suspicious-process-tree seed can expose real execution
   headroom, and the bounded `autonomous_gap_expansion` recipe closes that gap
   from catch-rate `0.086` to `0.143` on the tracked evasion corpus.
4. The checked-in benchmark artifact now distinguishes the flat
   production-like baseline from the narrower conservative-seed improvement
   claim, so operator-facing reporting does not overstate the result.
